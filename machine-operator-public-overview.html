讓新人訓練變得公平、清楚、有依據

Make training fair, clear, and evidence-based

這套系統協助工廠用分階段評分、訓練紀錄、獨立觀察與主管決策，把新人訓練轉成可追溯的人員安排依據。

This system turns phased scoring, trainer notes, independent observations, and manager decisions into a traceable staffing basis.
