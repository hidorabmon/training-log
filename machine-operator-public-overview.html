PUBLIC OVERVIEW · 大眾簡介

讓新人訓練變得公平、清楚、有依據

Make new-hire training fair, clear, and evidence-based

這套系統把現場訓練、每日紀錄、獨立觀察、AI 決策摘要與主管最終決策串在一起，讓每個人都知道現在在哪裡、下一步是什麼。

This system connects floor training, daily records, independent observations, AI decision briefs, and manager decisions so everyone can understand the current status and next step.

WHY IT MATTERS · 為什麼重要
WHY IT MATTERS

重點不是考倒新人，而是用清楚證據找到正確的培訓與職位安排。

The goal is not to catch people out. It is to use clear evidence to find the right training and role.

🛡️
更安全Safer在風險擴大前發現能力缺口。Find capability gaps before risk grows.
⚖️
更公平Fairer不只依靠單次面談或個人印象。Do not rely on one interview or impression.
🧭
更清楚Clearer每個人都知道下一步要做什麼。Everyone knows what happens next.

PEOPLE · 參與角色
PEOPLE

每個人只需要做好自己的部分

Each person owns one clear part

清楚分工讓訓練更穩定，也避免把所有壓力放在同一個人身上。

Clear responsibilities make training more consistent and prevent all pressure from falling on one person.

🧑‍🔧

Learns, practises, and demonstrates the work.

RESULTS

清楚的訓練安排

Clear training plan

知道由誰教、何時檢查，以及完成的標準。

AI 決策摘要

AI decision brief

幫主管快速看懂重點，但最終決策仍由主管留下理由。

Helps managers understand the key points; the final rationale is still recorded by the manager.

🤝

可解釋的人員決定

Explainable staffing decision

繼續、延長、複評、轉崗或不適合都有證據與下一步。

Continue, extend, reassess, reassign, or not-suitable decisions all have evidence and a next step.

這套系統的成功，不是要求每位新人一定通過；而是讓每個人都能在公平、安全、有依據的流程中，找到合適的下一步。Success does not mean every new hire must pass. It means everyone gets a fair, safe, evidence-based process to find the right next step.
