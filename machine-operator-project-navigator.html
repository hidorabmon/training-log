01 · Why

這不是一張評分表，而是一個低風險的判斷機制

This is not another scorecard; it is a low-risk decision mechanism

專案把現場教學、能力驗證、獨立觀察、AI 摘要與主管決策串成閉環；成功不等於每位新人都留下，而是更早知道適配與風險。

The project connects floor training, capability verification, independent observations, AI briefs, and manager decisions into a loop. Success means identifying fit and risk earlier.

Problem

主觀判斷不可追溯

Subjective judgment is not traceable

單次面談、口頭回答或一次成功操作，不能代表穩定掌握。

One interview, verbal answer, or successful attempt does not prove consistent mastery.

Method

用階段與證據取代印象

Replace impressions with phases and evidence

Phase 評分、Trainer Notes、Independent Reviews、AI Brief 與 Manager Decisions 共同形成決策依據。

Phase scores, trainer notes, independent reviews, AI briefs, and manager decisions form the decision basis.

02 · Architecture

資料從現場，流向可以被主管採用的決策

Data moves from the floor to a decision managers can act on

每一層只做一件事：前端記錄、後端權限、Sheets 留原始資料、Notion 分析、AI Brief 摘要、主管決策留痕。

Each layer owns one job: front-end capture, back-end authorization, Sheets source records, Notion analysis, AI Brief summary, and manager decision audit trail.
